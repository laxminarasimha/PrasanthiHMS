# Privacy policy for the Prasanthi Hospitals app

**Draft, written from what the app actually does. The hospital should read it, correct it, and have it checked before it is published.**

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This app is provided by Prasanthi Hospitals, Vijayawada ("the hospital", "we") for its patients, their families, and its doctors and staff.

## What we collect

- **When you register:** your name, mobile number, date of birth, gender and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled form; nobody at the hospital can read it.
- **Family members you add:** their name, date of birth, gender and how they are related to you.
- **When you use the app:** the appointments you book or cancel, and the monthly medicine lists you make.
- **Your hospital records:** prescriptions, lab reports and bills made at the hospital are shown to you in the app after the front desk has confirmed that the account is yours.
- **Staff:** doctors and staff sign in with the login the hospital gave them and see the patients and reports their work allows.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. It shows no advertisements and uses no advertising or tracking services.

## What we use it for

To book and manage your appointments, to keep your medicines ready, to show you your own medical records and bills, and to let your doctors care for you. We do not use it for anything else.

## Who sees it

Only the hospital's own doctors and staff, according to their work. We do not sell your information and do not share it with other companies, except where the law requires it (for example a court order or a public health authority).

## Where it is kept and how it is protected

Your information is kept on the hospital's own system. The app and the system talk over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Your sign-in is kept on your phone in its secure storage.

## How long we keep it

Your app login and the lists you made are kept until you close your account. Medical and billing records are kept for as long as the law requires hospitals to keep them.

## Your choices

- You can change your password in the app.
- You can remove a family member from your account in the app.
- You can close your account in the app (Account → Close my account). This removes your login, your family list and your monthly medicine lists. The hospital keeps its own medical and billing records of your visits, as it is required to.
- To ask for a correction to your records, or any question about your information, contact the hospital.

## Children

Parents or guardians may add their children as family members and manage their appointments and records. The app is not meant to be used by children on their own.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
