# Store listing

Text for the Google Play and Apple App Store pages. Change anything that does not sound like the hospital.

**App name:** Prasanthi Hospitals

**Short description (Play, up to 80 characters):**
Book appointments and see your reports at Prasanthi Hospitals, Vijayawada.

**Subtitle (App Store, up to 30 characters):**
Appointments and reports

**Full description:**

Prasanthi Hospitals, Vijayawada, in your pocket.

For patients and families
- Book an appointment with a doctor for yourself, your parents or your children, and see your token number.
- See today's reminders: an appointment coming up, a lab report that is ready, a visit your doctor asked for.
- Read your prescriptions and lab reports, once the front desk has verified your account.
- See your bills and what is still to be paid at the counter.
- Keep a monthly medicine list for diabetes, thyroid or blood pressure; the hospital pharmacy keeps the pack ready each month.
- Call the hospital or open the way in Maps.

For the hospital's doctors and staff
- The day's appointments and admitted patients.
- Lab results that need attention, and each patient's history at a glance.

Your medical records are shown only to you, and only after the hospital has confirmed the account is yours.

**Category:** Medical

**Contact email, phone and website:** to be filled in by the hospital.

**Privacy policy:** see `privacy-policy.md`. Both stores need it published at a public web address.

## Questions the stores will ask

- **Does the app collect personal data?** Yes: name, mobile number, date of birth, gender, and health information (appointments, prescriptions, lab results, medicine lists). It is collected to provide the hospital's services, sent encrypted (HTTPS), and not shared with or sold to anyone outside the hospital.
- **Can users delete their data?** Users can close their account inside the app (Account → Close my account). The hospital keeps its own medical and billing records, as hospitals must.
- **Does the app need a login to review?** Yes. The store reviewers need a test patient login and a test staff login. Make these on the live system before submitting, and remove them after approval.
- **Ads, payments, location, camera, contacts:** none.
- **Encryption:** only standard HTTPS (already declared in `app.json`).
